# Fresh test of the sealed carried-state response rule

**The unchanged carried-state response rule passes its frozen joint criterion
on 32/32 fresh literal contexts, with 8/8 in every schema/order stratum.** All
forecasts and their qualification were public before any native True measurement.
The completed original native audit and the separate cache-continuation wrapper
pass. This confirms prediction of a specified approximation correction at its
own input under the declared finite budget. It does not establish the complete
native learned generator, material margin necessity or a compact predictor.

## What was tested

The experiment tests the exact 283 response/products/random-writes rule, with no
retuned coefficient, additional iteration or changed criterion. B retains the
False-coefficient cubic at source MLP1 and MLP2. At its own carried corner xC,
P adds the finite-minus-cubic product R(xC), plus the staged gate-remainder and
up-branch responses to B's actual attention step. All 26 later source blocks,
all receiving computation, attention, normalization, projections, products and
readout remain finite. One extra finite corner and its Float64 staged derivative
are explicitly part of the prediction budget.

The new rhodonite/diopside literal instances form 16 dependent matched-schema
pairs. Their 64 input hashes are absent from the catalog of 2,048 historical
sequences. Selection used known inputs and tokenization, not model outcomes.
Templates, checkpoint and decoder are unchanged. This provides fresh-literal
support, not independent-template, checkpoint or research-team replication.

The five primary criteria remain joint: original native residual/verdict/margin
accuracy; full C1-B local write; its actual continued effect; paired C1-B local
schema contrast; and the conditional C1-K local increment. P must pass at least
24/32 and 6/8 in every schema/order stratum. All ineligible cases remain failures
in the denominator. Gate-only H, up-only J and norm-matched random increments
cannot rescue the P primary. Strict actual-increment and removal accounts retain
the separate .02-nat margin guard. Paired whole-token receiving contrasts remain
unavailable; only the source-write pair has a frozen coordinate correspondence.

## Fresh outcomes and the unresolved behavioral target

- P passes all five primary criteria **32/32**, including the conditional C1-K
  local increment and all **16/16** dependent paired schema-write contrasts.
  Every schema/order stratum is **8/8**, above the frozen 6/8 requirement.
- Carry-only K, gate-only H, up-only J, all three random increments and removal
  N pass the original native-response criterion **32/32**, but the conditional
  local increment **0/32**. Their joint counts are zero. H already passes the
  paired contrast 16/16, so that contrast alone does not require both terms.
- Full C1/U pass jointly 32/32 and are exactly equal. Baseline B passes native
  accuracy 16/32 and the joint test 0/32. Native T passes native accuracy 32/32
  but the conditional increment 0/32: its first source activation is finite,
  so it is not the positive control for the C1-K target.

P's median conditional local-increment error is **12.00%**, cosine **.99290**;
gate-only H has 48.96% error, up-only J 78.47% and random G0 140.49%. The full
missing local write has 1.58% median error; the paired local contrast 3.42%,
compared with K's 39.54%. On the actual full C1-B receiving effect, P's median
vector error is 1.59% and margin error .000572 nat.

P's median native receiving-vector error is .231%, verdict-vector error .184%
and actual finite-margin error .00234 nat. This is bounded native prediction
under the declared budget, but is not by itself evidence selecting the finer
response account: K and the other listed controls already pass that native test.

The strict incremental-effect account remains **0/32 for every arm**, including
exact C1/U calibration; the full-removal account also has 0/32 passes. No absolute C1-K margin effect reaches
the unchanged .02-nat guard; the maximum is about .01066 nat. Eligibility is therefore
0/32. We retain the frozen denominator and guard. Local vector success is not
evidence that the correction is materially necessary for the native margin.
Signed finite gate/up, nonadditivity and prediction-remainder ledgers remain
published; their projections are not causal fractions or learned features.

## A posthoc donor comparison limits case-specific interpretation

On these same fresh cases, substitute the P-K forecast write from the other
literal while holding schema, assignment, order and target fixed. The raw donor
passes the conditional local tolerance **30/32**, with 21.33% median error and
.97812 cosine. Matching its norm to the recipient's **forecast** norm also passes
30/32, at 20.87% median error; no native target norm supplies that scaling. The
own rule passes 32/32 at 12.00% error. Cross-schema donors with the other fields
fixed pass **0/32**, both raw and norm-matched. On 283's old cases, same-schema
cross-literal donors pass 32/32 at 17.62% raw error.
[Every comparison and provenance](../../results/carried-state-forecast-284/transport-posthoc/summary.json).

This shows substantial transfer of the local direction within these matched
schema/background conditions. The own rule gives finer precision, but the
25%/.95 tolerance does not establish that each case's particular computed vector
is necessary. It does not falsify the frozen 32/32 primary. The donor test was
designed **after** outcomes: it is a zero-forward comparison of saved write
vectors, not a new sealed predictor, causal installation or downstream native
test. Its 128 donor rows reuse 32 recipients in 16 dependent pairs. Neither
portability nor different sign regimes identifies learned features or a circuit.

## Completing native measurement without changing the forecasts

The original native phase measured C1/U/N/T on 17 complete cases: 68 full
forwards. It stopped at the frozen 512-MiB reserve before case 18, preserving
all 17 sealed case arrays and metadata. That partial set was not used to select
a rule, threshold or outcome claim.

The separately frozen 284b continuation changes only unused-cache handling and
resume qualification. It requires all 17 cases to reproduce byte-identical
arrays **and metadata** before measuring the remaining 15. A full unequal-token
CPU continuation fixture passed that replay and completed all 32 cases exactly;
its call to the unchanged original native audit also passed. This synthetic
qualification is not a real-checkpoint outcome.

That runtime qualification was frozen and published at
`8e2e4c3d5352f0490de69598a5e003dfca1ca88b` after the original stop and
before any continuation replay or new measurement. It is not misrepresented as
code frozen before the first 17 native cases.

The first two real continuation attempts stopped after model loading and before
any replay or new forward at the unchanged reserve. They add zero measurements
and remain preserved failures. A third attempt completed all 17 exact NPZ and
metadata replays **before** the remaining 15 measurements, in 40.80 seconds.
The original native audit, called unchanged through the separately frozen 284b
wrapper, then passed in 16.92 seconds: **1,024 required checks**, 128 finite
full-vocabulary heads and **32 ordinary True replays without editing hooks**.
The wrapper also validates the retained stop, original receipt and exact replay
hashes. Scientific sources, predictions, criteria, numerical scope, reserve and
forecast seal did not change.

Actual native producer work is **196 full forwards**: 68 from the interrupted
original, 68 exact replays and 60 new forwards. There are 32 unique native cases
and 49 ordinary True producer calls, including the 17 replays; the 32 unhooked
audit calls are additional. Unique completed study production is 320 forecast
plus 128 native-control forwards. Replays are not additional fresh support.

## What this advances in Question B

The rule predicts a local generated update across new literals without observing
their targets. Carry changes the operating point; the normalized gate/up
product's response there predicts a small attention modulation that the response
around False misses. Native accuracy, this conditional correction and its
material behavioral consequence remain separate questions.

All 26 later source blocks and receiving operations, plus the extra finite
corner, remain in the budget. The test does not establish useful compute savings,
which learned features generate the complete native direction, or transfer to
other templates/checkpoints. It is not a learned circuit, novelty-priority,
competent-verifier shutdown or complete Question B claim.
