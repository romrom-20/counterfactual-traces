# What survives, and where the investigation started to loop

**There has been real progress, and there has also been target drift.** The
strongest native findings constrain how an edited Boolean is communicated.
Several later studies instead explain the error of a chosen approximation.
Those are useful diagnostics, but repeatedly solving a new approximation's
error does not converge automatically on the native learned mechanism.

This is a reassessment of the existing reports and records, including the
[earlier research review](272-research-review-and-causal-baseline.md), native
interventions244–250, forecast sequence251–260, source studies273–285 and
response-map/direction tests286–295. It is not a fresh audit of every historical
line of code, an independent replication or a new literature review. Existing
verifiers preserve the evidence and its original failures.

## Three questions that should stop being conflated

**Can this late edit be predicted from one observed False computation?** Yes,
under a complete False-prefix KV information budget. [272](272-research-review-and-causal-baseline.md)
recomputes the affected suffix and reaches the native criteria on32 old cases,
using at most3.59% of a full forward's learned dense MACs, with2.94x median
measured marginal speedup on the recorded Mac. This is ordinary causal reuse,
not a discovered learned circuit. Its old raw-coordinate failures stay failed.

**Can an explicit nonlinear substitute generate the native response without
being supplied edited parents?** [292](292-joint-gate-forecast.md) says yes for
32 fresh literal pairs, under a substantially finite, dense network budget.
It predicts its own trajectory using a nearly exact rational activation update.
Its strict simpler controls fail. That is a bounded constructive result, partly
alternate numerical execution of a known primitive. It does not by itself
explain which learned representations produce the schema effect.

**Have we identified a small, transferable account of that particular learned
direction and its downstream consequence?** No. That is the unresolved part
of Question B. Neither of the preceding successes should be erased, and
neither should be expanded into this stronger answer.

## The strongest surviving constraints concern native computation

[248](248-native-carry-read-result.md) rejects carrying the changed verdict
state as a sufficient account. Later context matters. [250b](250b-native-source-communication-result.md)
adds a native primitive necessity constraint: continued source-value transmission
in the late region is consequential under the stated clamps. Direct reads,
relays and interactions remain distinct. It does not identify semantic features.

[251](251-fixed-receiver-attention-result.md) rejects frozen receiving attention
probabilities even when the source trajectory is exact. [252](252-source-driven-attention-result.md)
finds a much larger gain from adapting receiver queries than receiver keys;
every strict complete account there still fails. The architectural loop—messages
change a state, that state changes later reads—is permitted by transformers.
The quantitative intervention difference is the evidence, not novelty of the
loop itself.

[273](273-native-source-value-portability.md) finds that exchanging source-value
changes across schemas fails the stronger effect account, while preserving the
recipient directions and borrowing donor magnitudes passes a looser effect
criterion. [274](274-source-residual-reanchoring.md) rejects normalization
reanchoring as a sufficient explanation. Context dependence is consequential
upstream of that emission map. These are native direction constraints; they
are stronger mechanistic leads than the success of an arbitrary scalar fit.

[279](279-source-activation-forecast.md) predicts the first source MLP locally;
[280](280-source-composition.md) shows that uniformly repeating its cubic rule
fails, already at the next source MLP even with native incoming state. The
failure is not explained solely by inherited trajectory error. Yet repairing
one selected local discrepancy fails to restore the complete response.

[285](285-native-structure.md) transfers the first-source prediction across
four layouts. Borrowed directions nearly match too, with important margin
failures. [286](286-state-dependent-readout.md) and qualified
[287](287-source-receiver-readout.md) show why a small vector difference can
matter through a changed downstream sensitivity, and that the source can seed
that sensitivity shift. This is not evidence that source recurrence alone
accounts for subsequent receiving computation.

## Where the line became less informative

The correction factorials261–271 and carried-state work282–284 answer specified
questions about approximation discrepancies. Some fresh conditional predictions
pass, but the corrected increment can be below behavioral materiality and simpler
controls can already pass the native criterion. A successful discrepancy model
is not automatically a necessary explanation of the native verdict.

The receiving response-map sequence288–291 earns a conditional distinction:
receiving gated-MLP response matters after edited source, attention and head
are supplied. Partial coefficients cannot generally recover the internal
response. In291, a scalar calibration passes while most of the gradient and
receiving vector are wrong. That directly warns against treating a favorable
verdict number as mechanism identification.

[293c](293-response-subspace.md) and [294](294-response-change.md) reject
particular32-direction constructions. They do not show that the computation
has high intrinsic dimension: any single missing vector spans a line. The
meaningful question is how useful directions can be selected without observing
the missing vector. Their failed sufficient-account tests remain failed despite
weaker descriptive improvements. Original293's invalid baseline comparisons
remain explicitly invalid, with its read-only correction preserved.

There is a second risk: giving names to standard algebra as if it were a
learned discovery. Normalization geometry, gate–up product identities and
integrals of changing derivatives explain possible forms of state dependence.
Their relevance must be established by a discriminating native test. Reconstructing
a known function from its supplied endpoints is calibration, not that test.

## The change in research policy

295 is a final directed endpoint/path closure check for294's specific ambiguity.
Its outcome cannot license another automatic global repair, rank search or
quadrature sweep. Its [protocol](../experiments/295-finite-drive.md) was frozen
with that stopping rule. The [result](295-finite-drive.md) reports the full
local comparison separately from this reassessment.

Before another major run, write one competing claim about the native computation:
what learned messages or operations are sufficient, what alternative predicts
a different signed vector or finite margin, and which intervention separates
them. State every supplied state and computation. If it is a prospective claim,
freeze the rule before fresh native measurement; if it is retrospective, say so.
Compare prediction cost with causal-prefix reuse rather than a full sequence
that needlessly repeats unchanged positions.

For a learned-path claim, use direct primitive installation/removal and matched
controls on the actual native displacement, followed by a transfer test beyond
recycled literal variations. Large norms, sign regimes, scalar agreement or an
accurate supplied-state integral alone do not meet that bar. A selective
approximation can be useful without identifying such a path; those achievements
should have separate names and targets.

I would preserve the demonstrated computational constraints, close the latest
approximation-diagnostic line after295, and refrain from claiming a complete
mechanism. This is a change in direction justified by the record, not a reason
to discard the negative results or declare the broad question unanswerable.
